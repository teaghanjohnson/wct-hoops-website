how to add player pages and upcoming camps
==========================================

this folder holds two finished features that are not on the website yet.

  1. player pages: clicking a player on the proof page (alumni.html) opens a page
     with their story, timeline, quote and photos.
  2. upcoming camps: camp cards with dates, ages, price, spots left and a register
     button on the work page (bookings.html).

what is in this folder

  guide.txt              this guide
  player_template.html   the player page. you make one copy for each player
  card_snippets.html     the clickable player card and the camp cards, ready to copy and paste

the styles (how these pieces look) are already in the site at
site/assets/css/player-and-camp-cards.css. you switch them on page by page
in step 1 of each part below.


before you start
----------------

  * use a plain text editor. vs code is free and the easiest (code.visualstudio.com).
    textedit works too, but first go to format, then make plain text.
  * the pages you edit are in the site folder. files in this folder are only the
    templates you copy from.
  * anything in [square brackets] is a placeholder. replace the brackets and the
    words inside them with the real info.
  * only change the words between the tags. in <h4>Isaac Jack</h4> you change
    Isaac Jack and leave <h4> and </h4> alone.
  * every spot you need to edit is marked with a note that says edit:
    use cmd + f (mac) or ctrl + f (windows) to jump between them.
  * made a mistake? cmd + z (or ctrl + z) undoes it.
  * to check your work, double click the html file in the site folder and it opens
    in your browser. refresh the browser after each save.


part 1: player pages
--------------------

step 1: switch on the styles (only once)

  open site/alumni.html. near the top, find this line:

      <link rel="stylesheet" href="assets/css/style.css">

  right under it, paste this line and save:

      <link rel="stylesheet" href="assets/css/player-and-camp-cards.css">


step 2: make the player's page

  1. copy future_features/player_template.html into the site folder.
  2. rename the copy after the player: player_ then first and last name, all
     lowercase, with underscores instead of spaces. for example:

         player_isaac_jack.html

  3. put the player's photos in site/assets/img.
  4. open the new page and search for edit: to find each spot to fill in:
     the browser tab title, photos, name, level, current team, intro, quick facts,
     the journey timeline, the quote and the gallery photos.
  5. at the bottom, the previous and next links point to the players before and
     after this one. if they do not have a page yet, change the link to
     alumni.html or delete that line.
  6. save.


step 3: make their card clickable

  in site/alumni.html, find the player's current card. it is four lines long and
  looks like this:

      <div class="alum-card">
        <div class="alum-media"><img src="assets/img/alum-isaac.jpg" alt="Isaac Jack"></div>
        <div class="alum-body"><h4>Isaac Jack</h4><div class="school">Edmonton Stingers · Pro</div><div class="meta">Forward &middot; ex-Pacific University '26</div></div>
      </div>

  1. open future_features/card_snippets.html and copy everything between
     "player card: start copying here" and "player card: stop copying here".
  2. in alumni.html, select those four lines of the old card, delete them and
     paste the new card in the same spot.
  3. fill it in. the old card already had the photo, name, school and year, so
     copy those across. the href is the file name from step 2.

  here is isaac's card filled in, as an example:

      <a href="player_isaac_jack.html" class="alum-card" aria-label="View Isaac Jack's journey">
        <div class="alum-media">
          <img src="assets/img/alum-isaac.jpg" alt="Isaac Jack">
          <span class="alum-level">Pro</span>
        </div>
        <div class="alum-body">
          <h4>Isaac Jack</h4>
          <div class="school">Edmonton Stingers · Pro</div>
          <div class="meta">Forward &middot; ex-Pacific University '26</div>
          <div class="alum-cta">View Journey <span>→</span></div>
        </div>
      </a>


step 4: check it

  double click site/alumni.html. the card should lift and turn gold when you
  hover over it, and clicking it should open the player's page. on that page,
  the back to the proof button should bring you back.


good to know

  * only switch a card to the clickable version once that player's page exists.
    otherwise visitors click into a page not found.
  * cards with and without pages can sit side by side in the grid.
  * adding a brand new player: do steps 2 to 4, but paste the new card wherever
    you want them in the grid instead of replacing an old one. then update the
    featured alumni number near the bottom of alumni.html.


part 2: upcoming camps
----------------------

step 1: switch on the styles (only once)

  open site/bookings.html and add the same line under the style.css line,
  exactly like part 1, step 1:

      <link rel="stylesheet" href="assets/css/player-and-camp-cards.css">


step 2: paste the camp section

  1. open future_features/card_snippets.html and copy everything between
     "camp section: start copying here" and "camp section: stop copying here".
  2. in bookings.html, find this note:

         <!-- booking process: the four numbered steps -->

  3. click at the start of that line, press enter to make an empty line above it,
     and paste the camp section there. it now sits between the training options
     and the booking process.


step 3: fill in the camp cards

  the section comes with two example cards:

    registration open: gold label, dates, price, spots left bar and a register button
    coming soon: outline label, dates tba and a get notified button

  keep the one you need and delete the other (each card starts with a note that
  says camp card: and ends with its closing </div> before the next card).
  for more camps, copy a whole card and paste it right after the last one,
  still inside the section.

  fill in each spot marked edit:

    photo          a file in site/assets/img
    date box       short month (AUG) and the days (12–15)
    name and description
    ages, time and where
    spots left     the two numbers, plus the bar width. the bar width is how full the
                   camp is: 40 of 50 spots taken is 80%, so width:80%. delete the whole
                   spots left block if you do not want to show it.
    price
    register link  where sign ups happen: a google form link, a stripe payment link
                   made for that camp, or contact.html


step 4: point the camp buttons at the new section

  the camps box above the new section still has a button that goes to the contact
  page. in bookings.html, find:

      <a href="contact.html" class="btn btn-solid">View Upcoming Camps →</a>

  and change contact.html to #upcoming-camps so it becomes:

      <a href="#upcoming-camps" class="btn btn-solid">View Upcoming Camps →</a>

  on the home page (site/index.html), the view upcoming camps link goes to
  bookings.html#camps. change it to bookings.html#upcoming-camps so it lands right
  on the camp cards.


step 5: keep it up to date

  * a coming soon camp opens: delete its card and paste a registration open card.
  * a camp fills up: change the label text to Sold Out and change class="camp-status open"
    to class="camp-status soon". change the register button text or remove it.
  * a camp is over: delete its card.
  * no camps planned: leave one coming soon card up, or delete the whole section.


part 3: putting your changes online
-----------------------------------

editing the files on your computer does not change the live website. once your
changes look right in the browser, publish them the way README.txt describes
(the to publish part), or send the updated site folder to your developer.


if something looks wrong
------------------------

  the new cards look plain or unstyled
    the link line from step 1 is missing on that page, or has a typo.

  a photo does not show
    the file name after src= has to match the file in site/assets/img exactly,
    including .jpg versus .jpeg and capital letters.

  clicking a card says page not found
    the file name after href= does not match the player page in the site folder.

  everything below what you pasted looks broken
    part of a block was missed when copying. undo, then copy again from the
    start copying here note all the way to the stop copying here note.
