The Philosophy

The Roots
EST. 2020

Work Creates
Transformation.

WCT Hoops was created in 2020 by Ransford Brempong and Jarryn Skeete with a simple goal: add value back to the grassroots basketball community.

From the beginning, WCT has been about more than basketball — creating pathways for athletes to sharpen their skills, gain exposure at the highest levels, and connect with mentors who've already travelled the road. No shortcuts. Just work.
